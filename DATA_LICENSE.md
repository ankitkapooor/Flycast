# MaleCNS Dataset Attribution & Data License

The connectome data utilized by **FlyCast** is derived from the **MaleCNS v1.0** dataset, an open-access synaptic-resolution connectome of an adult male *Drosophila melanogaster* central nervous system.

---

## Authors & Collaborating Institutions

The MaleCNS dataset was generated and published by an international consortium of researchers:

- **HHMI Janelia Research Campus** (FlyEM Project Team)
- **University of Cambridge** (Department of Zoology)
- **MRC Laboratory of Molecular Biology** (LMB)
- **Google Research**

For complete scientific publications, methodology, neuron classifications, and data downloads, visit:
[https://github.com/flyem-male-cns](https://github.com/flyem-male-cns)

## FlyCast Disclaimer

FlyCast is an independent computational project and portfolio experiment. FlyCast is not officially affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by HHMI Janelia, the University of Cambridge, the MRC LMB, or Google Research.

No raw connectome data files (Feather / Parquet) are distributed within this Git repository. All connectome data is fetched dynamically at runtime via Google Cloud Storage or generated locally via synthetic test fixtures.
